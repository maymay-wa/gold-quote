Jeweler’s Helper

Support

Jeweler’s Helper prices a gold piece from its 3D model: weight in 9K, 14K or 18K gold, fine-gold content, and a quote from the live gold price, printing fee and VAT, in shekels or another currency you choose. The app is in English, Hebrew, Arabic, Russian, Spanish and French.

Opening a model

Share an STL file from Files, Mail, WhatsApp or AirDrop and choose Open in Jeweler’s Helper. You can also put STL files in Files > On My iPhone > Jeweler’s Helper.

How the weight is worked out

The weight comes from the model’s volume and the density of the chosen alloy. Sprues and casting loss aren’t included, so the cast piece can weigh a little more.

Prices

The gold price and exchange rate update automatically when you’re online. To quote with your own figures, enter them in Settings; leave them blank to go back to live prices.
